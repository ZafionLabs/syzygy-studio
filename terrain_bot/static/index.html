Terrain Bot: Earth, Moon and Mars look-alike terrain

Pick two bodies, then click anywhere on either map to find the places on the other one with the most similar terrain. Earth's underwater sea floor counts too. By default it uses real measured elevations: NOAA ETOPO1 for Earth (including the ocean floor), NASA LRO LOLA for the Moon, and NASA MGS MOLA for Mars. You can switch to your images, scaled from each body's real lowest point to its highest: Challenger Deep (−10,935 m) to Everest (+8,849 m) on Earth, Point Trieste (−9,129 m) to the Selenean summit (+10,786 m) on the Moon, and Badwater crater in Hellas (−8,201 m) to Olympus Mons (+21,229 m) on Mars. Or use the images with a brightness curve fitted to the real data.

Results similarity = 100 × exp(−distance); click a row to see why it matched
