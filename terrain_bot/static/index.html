Terrain Bot: Earth and Moon look-alike terrain

Click anywhere on either map to find the places on the other body with the most similar terrain. Underwater sea floor counts too. By default it uses real measured elevations: NOAA ETOPO1 for Earth (including the ocean floor) and NASA LRO LOLA for the Moon. You can switch to your images, where brightness is scaled so the darkest pixel is Challenger Deep (−10,935 m) and the lightest is Everest (+8,849 m) on Earth, and Point Trieste (−9,129 m) to the Selenean summit (+10,786 m) on the Moon.
