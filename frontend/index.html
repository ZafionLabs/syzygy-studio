Terrain Bot: Earth, Moon and Mars look-alike terrain

Find closest match by clicking the Moon or Mars. Compare two bodies by clicking either map. Earth's underwater sea floor counts too. By default it uses real measured elevations: NOAA ETOPO1 for Earth (including the ocean floor), NASA LRO LOLA for the Moon, and NASA MGS MOLA for Mars. You can switch to your images, scaled from each body's real lowest point to its highest: Challenger Deep (−10,935 m) to Everest (+8,849 m) on Earth, Point Trieste (−9,129 m) to the Selenean summit (+10,786 m) on the Moon, and Badwater crater in Hellas (−8,201 m) to Olympus Mons (+21,229 m) on Mars. Or use the images with a brightness curve fitted to the real data. The map buttons add temperature, ice and water, geology, weather, light, and radiation. Turn on more than one at a time. The percentage is how much of that map is mixed into the picture. Hover a selected point or a found match to see its name, a picture of the spot, the number-one match, the Moon place, and the match percentage. A click still searches by elevation.
